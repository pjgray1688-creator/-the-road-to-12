# Madhouse legacy payment reconciliation preview

The **Legacy payment reconciliation · read-only preview** on `/club/members/import` is a browser-local comparison aid. Load six files as six source rows: Club Manager memberships, Stripe customers and GoCardless customers for Rotherham and Carlton. Files are parsed in the browser tab only; no CSV contents are posted, saved, imported, or sent to Stripe/GoCardless. The established member-import workflow remains separate and is not invoked.

## Confirmed merchant and location model

- Current Stripe account labels are historical, not the future routing rule: **“Rotherham” is Madhouse Franchise Limited (`17383077`)** and **“Carlton” is Madhouse Products and Services Limited (`17383213`)**. The preview captures this historical label separately from site/location and resolves the known legal owner from it. Actual account ID/provenance is retained independently.
- Madhouse Franchise Limited receives **all new gym membership payments and renewals across both gyms**, irrespective of location. The gym/site and Club Manager `BranchId` remain separate identity/operational provenance and never choose the future Stripe merchant.
- Franchise also owns both historical GoCardless account contexts. Preserve the Rotherham and Carlton creditor/account provenance independently; do not collapse their provider IDs just because the legal entity is the same.
- Madhouse Products and Services Limited receives shop, supplement, merchandise, Glow Zone, PT and other non-membership sales. Its Stripe account is the future merchant for those transaction types.
- Club Manager remains responsible for existing collections until a separately approved cutover. No payment schedule is activated by this preview.

For the six source files, select each Stripe file's historical account label (`Rotherham` or `Carlton`) and enter its actual account ID/provenance. The two labels intentionally resolve to different legal entities; do not infer the site from the label. Label the two GoCardless exports separately with their historical site/account context and Franchise ownership. These are source/operator-supplied facts, not provider-verified facts.

## CSV parsing and review

Recognised identity/header variants include Club Manager `MemberId` / `Member ID` / `Club Manager Member ID`, `BranchId` / `Branch ID` / `Club Manager Branch ID`, and email/address variants; Stripe or GoCardless `id` / `Customer ID`, customer email, `Mandate ID` / `Mandate Status`, subscription/payment references, and flattened or JSON `Metadata` MemberId/BranchId. Schedule aliases include amount, frequency/interval, next payment/due date, paid-through/coverage end, collection/payment status. Unrecognised columns and missing required identity headers are listed per file in the preview. Rows with missing values remain visible as discrepancy issues when parseable; email-only overlaps are never confirmed links.

Membership source identity is the tuple `(site, BranchId, MemberId)`: BranchId and MemberId are distinct fields. Provider customer IDs are compared within `(provider, actual legal entity, explicit account provenance, customer ID)`, preserving the two Stripe owners and separate GoCardless creditor contexts. Repeated MemberIds, cross-site/branch appearances, duplicate emails, provider overlaps, missing identifiers, unmatched rows and ambiguous identities are reported for review. An **exact identity match** is only a source-key match. It does not prove active membership, a recurring arrangement, reusable payment authority, or successful collection; a Stripe customer row or historic payment reference alone proves none of those. Reports show identifier suffixes only, not names/emails or full customer/payment references. Mandate state, payment evidence and membership status are separate facts; an active mandate is not proof of paid/current membership.

If a matched member row and provider row include an explicit legacy billing/schedule reference, a Stripe arrangement under the Carlton-labelled MPS account is classified `LEGACY_ACCOUNT_EXCEPTION` with review severity. It remains observable, with actual merchant and references preserved; it is not automatically invalid and is never moved, cancelled, refunded, reauthorised or recreated. A matched MPS customer row without explicit arrangement evidence is reported as identity-only and does not receive the legacy-arrangement classification.

The repository did not contain the six real Madhouse exports at the time this change was prepared, so their exact headers and completeness remain unverified. The preview now reports unsupported/missing headers instead of silently implying full coverage. To test real files, open `/club/members/import`, add six source rows, choose the correct type and site, apply the provenance labels above, and inspect each file's header review before reviewing discrepancies. Keep the original exports access-controlled and do not include bank details or credentials.

For a local UI check, run `npm run dev` and open `/club/members/import` in the configured **non-production** development/test environment using an account already authorised for member import. Select the six files from the browser file picker; they stay in page memory. Do not configure local development against the production database. The isolated parser/dry-run tests run with `node --import tsx --test tests/legacy-payment-reconciliation.test.ts tests/legacy-stripe-billing-dry-run.test.ts`.

## Disabled Stripe membership schedule dry-run

The separate **Stripe recurring charge validation · disabled dry-run** uses the selected Club Manager membership CSVs and a proposed schedule CSV. It is strictly local and read-only: no API call, database read/write, import, provider reference persistence, outbox, arrangement, obligation, entitlement change or charge. A result marked validation-passed is **not authorization to charge**.

Each proposed **new** membership row must explicitly identify membership transaction type, receiving legal entity number `17383077`, site, BranchId and MemberId; the configured Franchise Stripe account; customer and payment-method account provenance; opaque `cus_` and `pm_` references; matching payment-method/customer relationship; amount/currency/frequency; next due/paid-through dates; membership and payment-authority state; explicit off-session authorization evidence; confirmed stopped Club Manager schedule; R12 arrangement/obligation and collection window; and any other provider schedule. All supplied account IDs must match the configured Franchise membership account. A known MPS account/legal-entity destination for a new membership proposal is classified `FUTURE_ROUTING_VIOLATION` and blocked. This future-routing rule does not invalidate legacy records in the reconciliation preview. Missing account provenance, ambiguous source identity, cancelled/inactive authority, incomplete schedules, missing authorization evidence, existing collectible schedules/obligations, or duplicate collection windows are also blocked. Account and provider references are never included in the downloaded dry-run report. Idempotency keys are deterministic opaque hashes scoped by organisation, legal entity, account, site/branch/member, period, amount and currency.

There is a pure proposal-policy guard for future basket design: membership lines map to Franchise and non-membership lines map to MPS; a proposed external basket containing both legal entities is rejected rather than routed wholly through one merchant. This guard is not connected to or changing the existing production checkout.

## Review and cutover checklist

1. Obtain approved, unmodified exports for both sites and providers; confirm supported headers and investigate all schema warnings before relying on counts. Verify each Stripe file's historical Rotherham/Carlton account label and legal owner without using that label as the member's gym location.
2. Resolve exact source identity conflicts against authoritative Club Manager records. Do not merge by email alone.
3. Independently verify Franchise/MPS account ownership and the correct Stripe account IDs, customer/payment-method ownership and relationship, current authority, off-session permission/consent evidence, currency/amount and any application-fee treatment.
4. Independently verify GoCardless creditor/account provenance for both historical contexts; do not infer mandate/payment state from customer overlap.
5. Reconcile full schedules, paid-through/collection status, arrears/cancellations and outstanding Club Manager collections. Agree a no-double-collection stop/start boundary.
6. Only after separate finance/operator approval should a shadow status sync or live cutover be designed. Existing Club Manager collections continue unchanged in the meantime.

The existing R12 one-off join flow and GoCardless recurring worker are not changed. Existing arrangements/obligations and imported `billing_evidence: not_imported` memberships are not modified or activated by this work. Provider-account and saved payment-authority persistence would require a separately reviewed server-side schema/security design; this preview does not persist references.
