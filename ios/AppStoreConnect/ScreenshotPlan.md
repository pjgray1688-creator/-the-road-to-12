# Screenshot capture plan

Capture the released build on real simulator/device UI with synthetic data. Do not composite features that are not in the submitted build. Keep status bars legible and ensure no real names, emails, balances or health data appear.

Apple currently accepts up to 10 screenshots per display size and requires at least one screenshot for each required display size. These projects support iPad; prepare a complete iPad set as well as iPhone. Current representative portrait dimensions are iPhone 1179 × 2556 or 1206 × 2622, and iPad 2064 × 2752 or 2048 × 2732. Confirm the eligible sizes in App Store Connect at upload time.

| Listing | iPhone sequence | iPad sequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R12 | Training plan; active session; progress chart; personal best; account | Training plan; session; progress; account |
| R12 Club | Venue selection; daily checklist with saved ticks; equipment status; fault report/history; member search; induction progress | Daily operations overview; checklist; issue escalation; member induction; audit attribution |
| R12 Coach | Client workspace; client programme; diary; programme builder; coach account | Client workspace; diary; programme; relationship-scoped client record |
| Glow Zone | Balance and expiry; activity; safety guidance; package information; account | Balance; activity; safety guidance; package information |

Use English (UK) copy and make each listing visibly distinct. Club images must show staff operations on the gym floor, not a member home screen. Avoid marketing captions inside screenshots until copy is approved.
